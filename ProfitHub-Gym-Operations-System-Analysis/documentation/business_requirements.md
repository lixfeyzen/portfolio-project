# Business Requirements

| ID | Business Requirement | Business Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-001 | Manage member records with contact, join date, and status. | Reduces paper dependency. |
| BR-002 | Track members as active, expired, unpaid, inactive, or expiring soon. | Improves clarity. |
| BR-003 | Make unpaid and overdue members visible to staff. | Reduces missed payment follow-ups. |
| BR-004 | Monitor monthly revenue from recorded payments. | Gives owners clearer business visibility. |
| BR-005 | Prevent class schedules from exceeding capacity. | Protects class quality and reduces disputes. |
| BR-006 | Prevent trainer double-booking across overlapping classes. | Improves schedule reliability. |
| BR-007 | Show owner summaries for members, payments, classes, and trainers. | Improves decisions. |
| BR-008 | Support reporting without manual spreadsheet consolidation. | Saves admin time. |
| BR-009 | Keep payment records consistent, searchable, and linked. | Supports auditability. |
| BR-010 | Provide role-based access for owners, admins, and trainers. | Limits sensitive data access. |
| BR-011 | Review membership, payment, and booking history by member. | Supports service. |
| BR-012 | Store audit details such as created date, updates, and staff user. | Improves accountability. |
