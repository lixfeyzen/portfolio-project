# Business Requirements

| ID | Business Requirement | Business Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-001 | The business must be able to manage structured member records, including contact details, join date, and status. | Reduces dependency on scattered spreadsheets and paper notes. |
| BR-002 | The business must be able to track each member's membership status as active, expired, unpaid, inactive, or expiring soon. | Improves daily operational clarity. |
| BR-003 | The business must reduce missed payment follow-ups by making unpaid and overdue members visible to staff. | Supports more consistent cash collection. |
| BR-004 | The business must monitor monthly revenue based on recorded payments. | Gives owners a clearer view of business performance. |
| BR-005 | The business must prevent class schedules from exceeding capacity. | Protects class quality and reduces front-desk disputes. |
| BR-006 | The business must prevent trainer double-booking across overlapping class schedules. | Improves trainer utilization and schedule reliability. |
| BR-007 | The owner must have visibility into active members, expired members, unpaid members, new members, class popularity, and trainer load. | Enables faster operational decisions. |
| BR-008 | The system must support operational reporting without manual spreadsheet consolidation. | Saves admin time and reduces reporting errors. |
| BR-009 | Payment records must be consistent, searchable, and linked to members and subscriptions where applicable. | Supports auditability and financial review. |
| BR-010 | The system must provide role-based access for owners, admins, and trainers. | Limits sensitive data access to appropriate users. |
| BR-011 | The business must be able to review member membership history, payment history, and class booking history. | Supports member service and issue resolution. |
| BR-012 | The system must maintain basic audit information such as created dates, updated dates, and staff user responsible for payment records. | Improves accountability for operational changes. |
