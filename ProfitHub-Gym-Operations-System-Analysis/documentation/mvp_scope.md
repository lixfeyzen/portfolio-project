# MVP Scope

## MVP Goal

The MVP goal is to define an internal gym operations system that helps staff manage members, subscriptions, payments, class bookings, attendance, and owner reporting with clear records and role-based access.

## Included Modules

1. Member Management
   - Add, edit, search, and review member records.
   - View membership, payment, and booking history.
2. Membership Plan & Subscription
   - Create plans and assign them to members.
   - Track start date, end date, status, and expiring memberships.
3. Payment Tracking
   - Record manual payments.
   - Track paid, unpaid, and overdue records.
   - Review payment history by member.
4. Class Schedule & Booking
   - Create classes, assign trainers, set capacity, book members, cancel bookings, and mark attendance.
   - Prevent expired member booking and trainer schedule conflicts.
5. Owner Dashboard
   - Show active members, expired members, unpaid members, monthly revenue, new members, popular classes, and trainer schedule load.

## Excluded Modules

- Member mobile app
- Trainer payroll
- Inventory management
- Complex accounting
- Loyalty points
- AI recommendation system
- Facial recognition attendance
- Payment gateway integration
- Multi-branch management
- Advanced CRM
- Marketing automation

## Why Member Mobile App Is Excluded

The MVP is focused on internal operational control. A member mobile app would add authentication, self-service booking, notifications, account management, and support requirements. Those features are useful later, but they would increase MVP scope before the gym has stabilized its internal member, subscription, payment, and schedule records.

## MVP Success Criteria

- Staff can create and update member records.
- Staff can assign membership plans and review subscription status.
- Unpaid and overdue payment records are visible.
- Expired members cannot be booked into classes.
- Trainer schedule conflicts are blocked.
- Owners can review key operational summaries in one dashboard.
- The system scope remains focused on internal gym operations.

## Future Enhancement Roadmap

- Member self-service portal or mobile app.
- Online payment gateway integration.
- Automated payment reminders.
- Trainer payroll and commission tracking.
- Inventory and retail product management.
- Multi-branch support.
- Advanced analytics and retention reporting.
- CRM campaigns and marketing automation.
