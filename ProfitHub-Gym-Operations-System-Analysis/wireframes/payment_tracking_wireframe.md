# Payment Tracking Wireframe Notes

## Page Purpose

Help admins record payments and quickly identify unpaid or overdue members for follow-up.

## Unpaid Member List

Columns:

- Member Name
- Current Plan
- Amount Due
- Due Date
- Payment Status
- Days Since Due Date
- Action

## Payment Record Form

Fields:

- Member
- Subscription reference
- Amount
- Payment method
- Payment status
- Payment date
- Due date
- Notes if added in future scope

## Payment History Table

Columns:

- Member Name
- Amount
- Method
- Status
- Payment Date
- Due Date
- Recorded By

## Overdue Indicator

- Highlight records where due date has passed and status is unpaid.
- Show a clear label such as Overdue.
- Keep this as an operational indicator, not an automated billing action.
